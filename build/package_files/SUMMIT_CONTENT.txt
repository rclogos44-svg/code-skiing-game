SUMMIT game content
===================

Game content in SUMMIT is original and was generated procedurally by the
game's own build tools:

- Mountain terrain: landscape-evolution simulation (uplift + stream-power
  erosion), particle hydraulic erosion and thermal erosion, written for SUMMIT.
- Textures (snow, rock, bark, noise, particles): procedural noise generators.
- 3D models (skis, poles, pine trees, park features, fences): generated in
  code at runtime. The rider's clothes are draped by a script in Blender
  (build/blender/build_rider.py).
- Rock shapes: scanned models from Poly Haven (polyhaven.com), CC0 / public
  domain - Boulder 01 by Rico Cilliers; Namaqualand Boulder 03 and 05 by
  Jenelle van Heerden and Dario Barresi; Rock 09 by Jenelle van Heerden.
- Animation: procedural (inverse kinematics) and a verlet ragdoll.
- Sound effects: synthesized from filtered noise and oscillators.

Apart from the CC0 rock scans above, no third-party art or audio is included,
and no proprietary game assets.
The engine (Godot Engine) and its third-party components are covered by the
licenses in GODOT_ENGINE_LICENSE.txt and THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt.
