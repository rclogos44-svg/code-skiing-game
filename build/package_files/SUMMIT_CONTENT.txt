SUMMIT game content
===================

All game content in SUMMIT is original and was generated procedurally by the
game's own build tools:

- Mountain terrain: landscape-evolution simulation (uplift + stream-power
  erosion), particle hydraulic erosion and thermal erosion, written for SUMMIT.
- Textures (snow, rock, bark, noise, particles): procedural noise generators.
- 3D models (skier, skis, poles, pine trees, rocks, chalets, chairlifts):
  generated in code at runtime.
- Animation: procedural (inverse kinematics) and a verlet ragdoll.
- Sound effects: synthesized from filtered noise and oscillators.

No third-party art, audio or proprietary game assets are included.
The engine (Godot Engine) and its third-party components are covered by the
licenses in GODOT_ENGINE_LICENSE.txt and THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt.
